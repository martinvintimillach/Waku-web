Our Story

From an idea to
a real mission

WÁKU was born from something simple: six friends who realized that helping others not only transforms the lives of those who receive help, but also the lives of those who give it.

We didn't start with resources or an established organization. We started with willingness, with time, and with the certainty that in the Amazonian and rural communities of Ecuador there were people who needed access to health, education and support that the State cannot always provide.

Today, WÁKU is a collective of committed volunteers — doctors, lawyers, educators and people from different professions — who invest their free time in bringing brigades to Shuar and Achuar communities in the Ecuadorian Amazon.

Questions
people ask us

Does 100% of my donation reach the community?

Yes, 100%. The collective's operational costs are covered by the founders themselves. This means every dollar you donate goes directly to funding medicine, materials, transportation and everything needed to reach the communities.

Why focus on the Amazon?

Amazonian communities like the Shuar and Achuar are among the most remote and with the least access to basic services in Ecuador. Their geographic isolation makes them especially vulnerable. That's why we started here — where the need is most urgent. But our vision is broader: we want to expand to rural communities across Ecuador as we grow.

How does a WÁKU brigade work?

Brigades are field operations where the team travels directly to communities — in many cases only accessible by small plane or canoe. Once there, we provide medical care, educational support and social assistance. The core team is the six founders, supported by external volunteers who have invested their time in joining this cause.

Are you an official organization?

WÁKU is a grassroots collective, legally established in Ecuador. We have a president, vice president, treasurer and secretary. We are transparent with fund management and accountable to our community of donors and volunteers.

Can I donate anonymously?

Of course. You can make your transfer without identifying yourself — simply leave the reference field blank or write any text. Your donation arrives just the same and is equally valuable. There is no need to identify yourself.

How can I volunteer?

You can sign up directly from our volunteer page. Your profession doesn't matter — what matters is your willingness to serve. We will contact you within 72 hours to guide you on upcoming brigades.

How to make the transfer?

1 Open your banking app or online banking
2 Select transfer to another bank or to Banco Pichincha
3 Enter the account details and the amount you wish to donate
4 Confirm the transfer — done! You are now part of WÁKU

Want to donate anonymously?

No problem. When making the transfer simply don't put your name in the reference field — you can leave it blank or write any text. Your donation arrives just the same and is equally valuable. You don't need to identify yourself.
